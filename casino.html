House Rules

  • Blackjack pays 3:2. A natural beats everything the Crown can hold.
  • Dealer stands on 17. No appeals, no remakes, no “one more card”.
  • Doubling is legal. Double your wager after the first two cards — one extra card only.
  • Splitting is royal. Two cards of equal value may be split into twin hands — one card each, no re-splitting. Aces receive exactly one card, as is proper.
  • Bust means bust. Gold over 21 stays on the felt. That’s how the mall gets funded.
  • The Crown shows mercy. Should your treasury run dry, petition once per session for a 100-gold relief grant.
  • Play money only. Imperial treasury, imaginary risk — zero real-world anything.
