Chronicles of the Empire

From a single colonial claim to dominion over the Caribbean, Central America, and Gran Colombia — the record of how the Crown was built.

Founding Era
Before the Crown

One Man, No Empire

Before there was a Colombian Empire, there was just ueq_ — alone, with nothing built and nothing to show for the time spent on the server. That changed the day he decided he'd had enough of standing still, and made a quiet vow: to build one of the greatest nations the server had ever seen. zattercal stood beside him from that very first day, and never left.

Founding Era
~3 Months Ago

Pijao Claimed as the Capital

Pijao wasn't picked at random. ueq_ is Native American, and Pijao's rich indigenous culture and community made it feel like home in a way no other location did. It became the first foundation stone of the Empire — and its capital ever since.

Expansion Era
2025 — Before the Empire

Tegucigalpa's Origins

Tegucigalpa existed before the Colombian Empire ever did. In 2025, zaity1 founded the nation of Honduras and built the town of Tegucigalpa long before ueq_ arrived on the server. The two weren't close at the time — but zaity1 would go on to become one of the Crown's closest allies, in a friendship neither of them saw coming.

Founding Era, cont.
2025

Salento Founded

zattercal — the oldest resident of Colombia itself, not just Central America — grew tired of his old town in the Dominican Republic. He moved south into Colombian territory to chase a new project: sandstone villas in Salento, built exactly the way he wanted them.

Founding Era, cont.
2025

Barbados Claimed

Crowlia — one of the kindest people in Colombia, always ready to lend a hand to anyone in the nation or across Central America — claimed Barbados for one simple reason: he loves islands, and Barbados was the perfect one for him.

Governance
The Imperial Activity Law

Cutting Inactivity, Not Morale

ueq_ removed roughly 20% of the population for inactivity. No one was upset about it — the nation understood why. The belief inside Colombia has always been simple: everyone gets a real chance to help build what the nation deserves to be. What you do with that chance is up to you.

Present Day
Now

Built for the People Who Show Up

The Empire isn't the biggest it's ever been, but it's lively in the way that actually matters — people who enjoy the time they spend here. Right now the focus is automation and spawner farms, built to make the Pijao shops faster and more profitable for the traffic coming through.

"We don't just build; we engineer civilizations."

— The Crown
